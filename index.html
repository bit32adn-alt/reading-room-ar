Reading Room AR

Two devices. One phone shows the room photo; the other films it and stands the 3D room on top.

1 · Phone in your handShows the room photo full screen. Turn brightness up. 2 · Filming phone or tabletOpens the camera and tracks the other phone. Start your screen recording here.
  1. Open 1 on the phone you will hold, and tap Full screen.
  2. Open 2 on the other device and allow the camera.
  3. Point it at the held phone from about 30–50 cm away. The room appears on the phone; tilt and turn the phone slowly.

Table version places the room on a table instead, with one phone.
